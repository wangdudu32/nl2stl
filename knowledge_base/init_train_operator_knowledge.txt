meaning: "φ since ψ 表示过去某个时刻 ψ 成立，并且从那以后 φ 一直成立到当前。"
cases:
  - semantic: "有界过去窗口内存在起点事件，之后左侧条件持续保持到当前。"
    cue: "x has been remaining ... since there is sometime during the last a to b time units once, at which y keeps ...：since 用过去窗口限定起点。"
    pattern: "φ since [a:b] ψ"

  - semantic: "无界过去范围内的 since 关系，从过去某个起点开始左侧条件一直维持到当前。"
    cue: "x remained ... since y was value, which would once get detected at a certain moment in the past from the beginning of the execution：起点不受有限窗口限制。"
    pattern: "φ since ψ"

===== since_fall =====

operator: since_fall
meaning: "φ since(fall(ψ)) 表示左侧条件从过去某个 fall 事件发生后持续到当前。"
cases:
  - semantic: "有界过去窗口内的 since 起点是一个 fall 事件。"
    cue: "x has been in a mode consecutively since once y deviates from value at a certain moment within the last a to b time units：since 的历史锚点是 fall。"
    pattern: "φ since [a:b] (fall(ψ))"

===== since_not_fall =====

operator: since_not_fall
meaning: "φ since(not fall(ψ)) 表示左侧条件从过去某个没有发生 fall 的锚点后持续到当前。"
cases:
  - semantic: "有界过去窗口内的 since 起点是一个 not fall 条件。"
    cue: "x has been uninterruptedly holding like that since y didn't go lower than c, which once got detected at a time before between a to b time units：since 的历史锚点是 not fall。"
    pattern: "φ since [a:b] (not fall(ψ))"

  - semantic: "无界过去范围内的 since 起点是一个 not fall 条件，左侧条件从该锚点后一直持续到当前。"
    cue: "the left condition has held since a past moment where the target fall event did not occur：since 的无界历史锚点是 not fall。"
    pattern: "φ since (not fall(ψ))"

===== since_not_rise =====

operator: since_not_rise
meaning: "φ since not rise(ψ) 表示过去某个时刻没有发生 ψ 的 rise 转移，并且从那以后 φ 一直成立。"
cases:
  - semantic: "过去有界窗口内的 since 起点是一个被否定的 rise 事件，起点之后左侧条件持续保持到当前。"
    cue: "x has not been above a threshold since there was a moment in the past window at which y entering a closed range was not observed：since 的历史起点是 not rise。"
    pattern: "φ since [a:b] (not rise(ψ))"

===== since_rise =====

operator: since_rise
meaning: "φ since rise(ψ) 表示过去发生过 ψ 的 rise 转移，并且从那以后 φ 一直成立。"
cases:
  - semantic: "过去有界窗口内的起点是 rise 事件，起点之后左侧条件持续保持到当前。"
    cue: "x remains ... since y entered the bound (a, b), which took place at a time during the elapsed window：since 的历史起点是进入事件。"
    pattern: "φ since [a:b] rise(ψ)"

  - semantic: "无界过去范围内以 rise 事件作为 since 起点。"
    cue: "x remains ... since the scenario that y got to at least c once occurred at a certain time instant in the past starting from the beginning of the execution：从 rise 起点延续到当前。"
    pattern: "φ since rise(ψ)"

===== until =====

operator: until
meaning: "φ until ψ 表示 φ 需要持续保持，直到 ψ 在未来某个时刻成立。"
cases:
  - semantic: "有界 until 响应要求左侧条件持续保持，直到右侧目标在指定未来窗口内出现。"
    cue: "needs to be ... and should hold like that consistently until finally at a certain moment within the first a to b time units ...：左侧保持到右侧在窗口内出现。"
    pattern: "φ until [a:b] ψ"

  - semantic: "无界 until 要求左侧条件从当前持续保持，直到未来某个未指定上界的时刻右侧条件成立。"
    cue: "consecutively ... ought to keep ... until at sometime in the future before the end of the execution ... eventually：右侧最终出现前左侧不能中断。"
    pattern: "φ until ψ"

===== until_fall =====

operator: until_fall
meaning: "φ until fall(ψ) 表示左侧条件持续保持，直到右侧 fall 事件发生。"
cases:
  - semantic: "有界 until 的终止条件是某个 fall 转移事件。"
    cue: "ultimately at sometime during the following b time units the scenario that x starts deviating from value takes place, and before that y keeps holding：右侧不是普通状态，而是 fall 事件。"
    pattern: "φ until [a:b] fall(ψ)"

  - semantic: "无界 until 的终止条件是未来某个 fall 转移事件。"
    cue: "x remains above a threshold continuously until the state of another signal deviates from a value sometime in the future：until 的右端目标是无界未来中的 fall 事件。"
    pattern: "φ until fall(ψ)"

  - semantic: "无界 until 的终止条件是信号离开指定区间的 fall 事件。"
    cue: "x keeps satisfying the left condition until y gets out of the range [a, b] at a future moment：右端 fall 表示区间谓词失效。"
    pattern: "φ until fall(lower <= y <= upper)"

===== until_not_fall =====

operator: until_not_fall
meaning: "φ until(not fall(ψ)) 表示左侧条件持续保持，直到某个满足时刻上 ψ 的 fall 转移不发生。"
cases:
  - semantic: "无界 until 的终止条件是某个 fall 事件被否定。"
    cue: "x remains set to a value until eventually another signal does not leave a closed range at a future time：until 的右端目标是 not fall。"
    pattern: "φ until (not fall(ψ))"

  - semantic: "有界 until 的终止条件是某个 fall 事件被否定，左侧条件持续保持到该窗口内的满足时刻。"
    cue: "the left condition stays continuously until the target signal does not begin deviating at a time within the future window：until 的右端目标是有界 not fall。"
    pattern: "φ until [a:b] (not fall(ψ))"

===== until_not_rise =====
