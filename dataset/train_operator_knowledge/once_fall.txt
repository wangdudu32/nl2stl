operator: once_fall
meaning: "once(fall(φ)) 表示过去某个时间点曾经发生过 φ 的 fall 转移。"
cases:
  - semantic: "过去有界窗口内曾经发生离开开区间的 fall 事件。"
    cue: "x went out of the bound (a, b) once at a certain time instant during the last a to b time units：once 的目标是 fall 事件。"
    pattern: "once [a:b] (fall(φ))"

  - semantic: "过去有界窗口内曾经发生等值或模式关系偏离的 fall 事件。"
    cue: "at a certain moment within the last b time units the transition action that the mode of x begins deviating from value will get observed once：once 的目标是等值关系的 fall。"
    pattern: "once [a:b] (fall(x == value))"

  - semantic: "过去有界窗口内曾经发生离开闭区间的 fall 事件。"
    cue: "there once existed a past moment within the window where x got out of the range [a, b]：once 回看窗口内是否出现过区间谓词失效事件。"
    pattern: "once [a:b] (fall(lower <= x <= upper))"

  - semantic: "从执行开始到当前的过去范围内曾经发生过指定 fall 事件。"
    cue: "once at some time in the past, the target signal fell out of the specified range"
    pattern: "once(fall(φ))"
