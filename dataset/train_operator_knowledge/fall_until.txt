operator: fall_until
meaning: "fall(φ until ψ) 表示一整段 until 条件从 true 转为 false。"
cases:
  - semantic: "持续-直到关系整体失效。"
    cue: "the following condition has to change from true to false: x is consecutively equal to value till at a certain moment in the following a to b time units ...：fall 作用在整个 until 模式上。"
    pattern: "fall(φ until [a:b] ψ)"

  - semantic: "当 until 左侧包含 not fall 约束时，fall 表示这整段持续禁止关系整体失效。"
    cue: "the condition that x will always not become less than or equal to c till y is finally observed must be detected to change from true to false：fall 的对象是包含 not fall 的 until 模式。"
    pattern: "fall((not fall(φ)) until [a:b] ψ)"
