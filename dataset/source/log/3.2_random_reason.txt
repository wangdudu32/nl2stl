Random 100 DeepSTL template sampling rationale
==============================================

Source
Samples were drawn from template_difficulty_level.txt after it had been sorted by difficulty level and outside-to-inside operator signature.

Sampling goal
Select 100 templates while balancing complexity coverage and structural diversity. The output random_100_template.txt contains only template text, one template per line.

Reproducibility
Random seed: 20260603.

Complexity quotas
The sample intentionally includes every difficulty level C1-C7. Quotas give enough examples to simple levels while reserving most slots for the dense middle/high-complexity regions.
C1 atomic_temporal: selected 8 of 12; quota 8; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 6.
C2 basic_unary_or_window: selected 10 of 50; quota 10; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 33.
C3 simple_boolean_or_trigger: selected 14 of 119; quota 14; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 85.
C4 standard_composite: selected 18 of 225; quota 18; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 177.
C5 nested_temporal: selected 20 of 311; quota 20; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 217.
C6 complex_event_until_since: selected 18 of 234; quota 18; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 209.
C7 extreme_composite: selected 12 of 87; quota 12; available outside-to-inside operator-signature groups 84.

Diversity rule inside each level
Within each difficulty level, templates were grouped by outside-to-inside operator signature. The signature records operators by parenthesis depth, comparing shallower operators before deeper operators and preserving left-to-right order at the same depth. One median-score representative was sampled from as many signature groups as possible; if the level quota was not full, remaining slots were filled randomly from unselected templates in that level.
